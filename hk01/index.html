Mobile: vertical in the article, horizontal in full screen手機版：文章內直式，全螢幕橫式

  1. In the article there's one vertical player and a floating Divee bubble in the corner.
  2. Tap the bubble, or Ask on the video, to open the full-screen view.
  3. The same player turns horizontal at the top and keeps playing. The chat sits below it, with the publisher's promo slot at the top of the chat.
  4. Each question plays a matching video, with its preroll, in the player above.
  5. On the first question the rewarded wall opens: the message in the chat, then a full-page ad. The floaters stay on top. The player and the promo slot stay live.
  6. Close, and the player goes back into the article as a vertical player.
  1. 文章內有一個直式播放器，角落有浮動的 Divee 按鈕。
  2. 點按鈕或影片上的提問，開啟全螢幕畫面。
  3. 同一個播放器轉為橫式置頂並持續播放，下方是聊天，聊天最上方是出版商的推廣版位。
  4. 每個提問都會在上方播放器播放相關影片（含片頭廣告）。
  5. 回答兩題後，激勵廣告牆只蓋住聊天區，播放器與推廣版位持續運作。
  6. 關閉後，播放器回到文章中，恢復直式。

Mobile anchored: the player and Divee as one unit手機嵌入式：播放器與 Divee 合成一個版位

  1. One in-article unit: a 16:9 player with the anchored Divee unit directly below it, at full content width.
  2. Before anyone asks, Divee shows the AI summary, the promo slot and suggested questions.
  3. Each question plays a matching video, with its preroll, in the player right above the answer.
  4. After the first question the summary goes; the promo slot stays at the top of the chat.
  5. On the first question the rewarded wall opens: the message in the chat, then a full-page ad. The floaters stay on top. The player keeps playing.
  6. No overlay and no tap to open: it reads like part of the article, which suits publishers who don't want floating units.
  1. 一個文章內版位：16:9 播放器，正下方是嵌入式 Divee，滿版寬度。
  2. 提問前，Divee 顯示 AI 摘要、推廣版位與建議問題。
  3. 每個提問都會在答案正上方的播放器播放相關影片（含片頭廣告）。
  4. 第一次提問後摘要收起，推廣版位留在聊天最上方。
  5. 回答兩題後，激勵廣告牆只蓋住聊天區，播放器持續播放。
  6. 不需浮層也不需點開：看起來像文章的一部分，適合不想要浮動版位的出版商。

  5. No Promote slot and no sponsored suggestions in this package. The rewarded wall works as usual.

  4. 回答附上商品卡，商品影片在上方播放器播放。

What happened, step by step發生了什麼（逐步紀錄）

Each question is tagged with a topic and an intent, the Player's ad request carries them, and a matching video plays.每個提問都會標記主題與意圖，播放器的廣告請求帶上這些標記，並播放相關影片。

    Same flow in every format所有形式都是同一套流程

    • Preview shows the AI summary and the promo slot. After the first question the summary goes away. The promo slot stays and the chat continues.
    • Rewarded wall on the first question: the publisher's "Dear readers" message, then a rewarded ad that can't be skipped, then the chat stays open for the rest of the conversation (one ad per chat). The ad covers the whole page; the floaters stay on top.
    • Switches turn the AI summary, the promo slot, sponsored suggestions and the rewarded wall on or off. The commerce tabs never show the promo slot or sponsored suggestions; the product bar switch applies only to them. Brand takeover turns sponsored suggestions on and the wall off by default; you can change either.
    • 預覽顯示 AI 摘要與推廣版位。第一次提問後摘要收起，推廣版位保留，繼續對話。
    • 激勵廣告牆在第一次提問時出現：出版商訊息，接著一則不可略過的全頁激勵廣告，之後本次對話全面開放（每次對話一則廣告）。浮動元件不蓋。
    • 開關可分別開啟或關閉 AI 摘要、推廣版位、贊助推薦與激勵廣告牆。商品方案分頁不顯示推廣版位與贊助推薦；商品縮圖列開關只適用於商品方案。品牌包版預設開啟贊助推薦、關閉廣告牆，兩者都可以再調整。
